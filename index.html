JobTrail HK

A job application organiser for people tracking their job search in Hong Kong.

JobTrail HK helps users save job information, track application status, keep notes and cover-letter text, and preview supported job listings.

Launch status

The multi-user web application is being prepared for launch. This public information site does not provide account registration or access to job records.

Account email

The application sends email-verification and password-reset links from the JobTrail sender account. Gmail API integration is being prepared. Google authorization is for the operator's sending account, not recipients of these emails.
